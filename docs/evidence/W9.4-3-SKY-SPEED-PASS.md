## Mission

Fast Sonic-like physics ball on a floating sky ribbon, with curved
turns, magnetic boost strips and bounded magnetic safety arcs.
The signature input is UPWARD FLICKS that **physically add momentum**;
sideways drag remains steering. No fake animation-only speedups.

## Prototype test mode

`/ball/?mode=speed`. Original `/ball/` physics and original 2D/3D
Mass Runner remain unchanged. Private ITHappy visuals remain opt-in
`?art=licensed` for owner-only local preview, never publicly distributed.

- Continuous 180m winding ribbon made from small overlapping **static**
  Bullet planks; no engine rewrite, no new collider dependency.
- 3 physical momentum boosts triggered on swept pad crossing.
- 2 localized magnetic safety arc sections; proportional inward forces
  near edges, no forced teleport/steering autopilot.
- 52m/s initial tunable empirical cap (187 km/h), **not a PlayCanvas
  or device maximum**. Increase only after robust browser/Android evidence.
- User vertical flick gives mass-scaled dynamic rigidbody impulse and
  increases sustained cruise. Sideways pointer drag controls lateral
  force relative to track centerline.
- Follow camera widens FOV with speed; sky background, clouds beyond
  play corridor and **no roadside trees**.
- Previous W9.4-2 optional licensed GLB ball skin retained. Old road
  GLB not stretched across bends; curved ribbon has lightweight proxy
  art until a genuinely curved model is approved.
