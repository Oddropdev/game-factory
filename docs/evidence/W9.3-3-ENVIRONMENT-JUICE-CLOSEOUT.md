## Rendered visual changes and review

Actual W9.3-3 screenshots were extracted and inspected, including portrait ready,
portrait approach/post-gate, landscape approach and small Android result. W9.2D
baseline screenshot artifact `11543529386` and W9.3-2 screenshot artifact
`11549673401` were also inspected.

- 46 actual rounded sphere meshes make a cohesive, symmetric soft garden:
  gentle hills, organic canopy/trunks, cotton clouds and sparse colored dots.
  Previously visible angular Kenney trees and flag no longer populate the scene.
- Mint/cream/coral/lilac/sky palette now extends across scenery, track and
  props; specular and hard shadow contrast restrained.
- A pooled 12-bead expanding ring and lightweight avatar squash impulse
  mark **actual** collision/gate results rather than missed orbs.
- +/− gate operators stay spatially anchored and readable at portrait and
  landscape sizes, including zero-clamped mass arithmetic.
- HUD now calls out the one-level prototype honestly and retains pointer-transparent
  surfaces, accessible START/RETRY and viewports without horizontal scroll.

**Visual limitations:** soft trees and clouds remain highly procedural and
stylized; there is still substantial sky negative space and limited environment
variety. Material/lighting and visual quality are better than W9.2D but do not
constitute an owner-approved 4/5 mobile game. Real screenshots are from different
elapsed frames, so side-by-side is qualitative, not frame-perfect proof.
