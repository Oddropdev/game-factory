# W9.4-5.1 — Green Rail Priority & Edge Steering

Status: IMPLEMENTATION; browser/Android acceptance PENDING
Source of truth: main `95f06b070df888dd74a268129a6ce445359d5851`.

## User-observed bug

The original safety field pushed balls toward the center if the newer
rail magnetic field temporarily returned null near its activation bounds.
Both old cosmetic side rails and physical green rails overlapped.
The original lateral gesture range was limited to +/-3.3m, yet the
physical green rail contact centerline is approximately 3.8m from the
ribbon's center. This made player-chosen high-speed rail riding harder
than intended.

## Minimal mechanical fix

- **Rail precedence is decided at section level**, not only when the
  ball is inside the narrow rail magnetic field. On 29–81m, 100–157m
  and 339–394m, the old inward safety force is suppressed; no force
  competes against the rail while the player is approaching it.
- W9.4-5.1 `?mode=rail` expands lateral steering target range
  from +/-3.3 to +/-4.18m. All other gameplay modes keep +/-3.3m.
- Hide overlapping decorative legacy rails on the outside edge at
  the physical green rail, on both original 174m and extended 440m
  sections. Underlying Bullet track geometry is unmodified.
- Preserve separate pre-contact attraction, limited downforce,
  contact-only forward boost, green emissive glow and pooled sparks.
  No forced teleport, magnetic autopilot, particle spawner redesign,
  speed-cap change, licence upload or Pages deployment.
- Unrelated non-rail bends preserve their existing old safety force.
