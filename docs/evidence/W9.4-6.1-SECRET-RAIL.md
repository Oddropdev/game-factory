Status: **Browser implementation and acceptance in progress**. Source of truth: accepted main W9.4-6 merge `39e53b4b6e09ff546534c3aad323b4d7be4a3d3e`.

## Mission: optional means optional

Prior W9.4-6 had an elevated 344–402m secret rail that was only geometry. At first it crossed the centerline at ~341m, blocking the normal main-road route, and player steering into it achieved ZERO top-grind support. Raising the ramp from the center forced the ball upward and led to ~8m airborne height instead of real train-rail riding. These failures were observed in prospective Chromium physics tests, not inferred from a drawing.

## Small focused correction

- Move the narrow secret rail to a left-hand player-selectable lane; the third green magnetic GUARD stays on the right and remains a separate side/top boost.
- Retain the existing 19m **floorless bridge**, seven boost pads, 440m course and 64m/s prototype speed cap.
- A real 32m sloped static Bullet entrance in the left lane supports the rise onto the secret TOP. No teleport; no forced mode switch; center lane runs underneath/unaffected.
- Once the player deliberately holds the left lane during the secret approach, a finite Bullet force keeps the ball near the rail in X and resists ballistic upward overshoot. These forces cannot boost or award GRIND; **a true named upper collider contact plus valid ball height/lateral position remains mandatory**.
- The secret rail then ascends a further 2.5m and descends along the existing spline. The normal main road underneath stays available.
- No GLBs or original ITHappy art in the repo, no public Pages change.
