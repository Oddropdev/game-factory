## Objective

Test a longer **440m** Sonic-fast rolling-ball course. Preserve both
accepted `/ball/` and `/ball/?mode=speed` as controls. The new private
experimental route is `/ball/?mode=jump`.

## Physics contract

- Continue using a single PlayCanvas Ammo/Bullet dynamic rigidbody ball.
- Add real static sloped rigidbody launch ramp, a literal unsupported gap,
  and separate 71m x 14m wide static landing platform. It must physically
  collide. Visual bands/rails are not the contact physics.
- Crossing the lip applies one mass-scaled *vertical* Bullet impulse,
  conditioned on the real ball position; all forward velocity is preserved.
- Landing is counted ONLY on a `collisionstart` callback from the
  `long-landing-deck` rigidbody after takeoff, never by comparing positions.
- Four additional booster strips and landing-edge magnetic safety graphics.
- Upward swipe still increases actual momentum; lateral drag still steers.
- Initial longer-route speed trial cap **64m/s (230.4km/h)**, versus 52m/s
  for original W9.4-3. Not evidence of a stable maximum on physical Android.
- Remain wholly optional. No owner ITHappy GLBs or private archives in repo.
