## Real floorless segment

The 440m test course has a mode=grind-only **19m cutout**
at progress 198–217m. No underlying broad track is generated over that
span: one narrow raised train rail carries the dynamic ball across.
An actual sloped approach ramp is used, not an invisible teleport.
Previous `?mode=jump` and `?mode=rail` preserve their original surfaces.

## Optional elevated secret route

At progress 344–402m, a distinct narrow steel rail ascends up to **2.5m
above** its entry elevation, curves away from the main road by up to
3.1m, then descends. The broad main road remains below; the route is
independent physics geometry, not a painted illusion. Entering and
leaving it will require player-feel review.

  reattachment cooldown prevents fighting input.
- GRIND cannot lock/boost on sides, with a mandatory named top collider
  contact and ball above/near the narrow running top.
- Actual ball travels across floorless gap using train rail, no checkpoint.
- Separate viewport 390×844, 360×800, 844×390 smoke captures.
- Old physics, 174m, 440m, and W9.4-5 rails regressions unaffected.
