### Defect found and corrected before closing this phase

On the prior high-DPI screenshot the +0 / +3 labels floated above and left
of the actual portal arches, even though their glyphs, colors and bounds
satisfied the original tests. The PlayCanvas `worldToScreen` points were
already CSS viewport coordinates; dividing by `device.maxPixelRatio`
displaced HTML labels when DPR > 1. This was fixed in the
game-local `spikes/w9-playcanvas/src/main.ts` without any change to model
state or gate collision.

W9.3-4 now explicitly compares each label's DOM center against its
independent projected screen point with a <=2 CSS px tolerance at DPR1
and DPR2. The **new real DPR2 screenshot visibly puts both labels
directly over their corresponding portal heads**.

The prior issue of lingering reward text behind result dialogs was also
removed via game-local UI presentation. Both success and failure result
screens show their buttons and main message without stale text in the foreground.

### Remaining limitations (not ignored)

- World retains sparse repeated candy-colored trees, cloud puffs, simplistic
  track depth and limited scenery variation. These are product-level aesthetic
  tradeoffs, **not** a new system-architecture backlog.
- Landscape is a fallback: compact hierarchy and less space for the avatar.
- The scripted browser captures show a working visual/interaction path,
  but cannot confirm physical Android perceived FPS, gestures, audio,
  heat, browser notches or subjective art acceptance.
- Owner has not yet given the five individual 1–5 ratings or overall >=4/5
  and comparative better-than-W9.2D confirmation.
