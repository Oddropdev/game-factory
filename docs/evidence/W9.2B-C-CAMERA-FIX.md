## Reported problem

The owner's real recording of the newly deployed C variant showed
almost nothing but dark navy background and a narrow strip of track near the
bottom. The previous W9.2B CI passed because it checked that WebGL2 rendered
without errors, not that the **gameplay world was visible**.

This was a genuine release-validation escape; the original W9.2B technical PASS
did not establish product visibility.

## Root cause and repair

Shader virtual camera used reversed pitch:
`ey=dy*.958+dz*.287, ez=-dy*.287+dz*.958`.

That projects the player at or beyond the bottom of the viewport. The
correct downward-facing camera uses:
`ey=dy*.958-dz*.287, ez=dy*.287+dz*.958`.

The scene floor and side rails now stop **in front of the camera**, and the
perspective divide uses actual view-space w without clamping geometry behind
the camera into the view volume.

## Acceptance regression

In addition to preserving existing WebGL2/depth/touch tests, Playwright
captures **real game screenshots** in portrait and landscape and samples the
central visible region, asserting non-background geometry is present.

Measured from the archived previous W9.2B and fixed CI screenshot evidence:

- old portrait central distinctiveness: 0.0
- repaired portrait: approximately 0.887
- old landscape: 0.0
- repaired landscape: approximately 0.372

These measurements were made for the viewport centre region, excluding
HUD and bottom strip. New CI enforces >0.08 distinctive fraction for both
orientations. This is a functional *visibility* gate, not an artistic-quality
or mobile frame-rate certificate.
